# Research summary and strategy recommendation, 2026-10-02

Four bounded research passes (published evidence, practitioner rules, risk practice, open-source
tooling). Every claim below carries a source. Nothing here is a promise of profit. Paper results,
even on real quotes, never establish live profitability.

## 1. What the evidence says about buying 0DTE SPY/SPX options

- Unconditional long 0DTE positions have negative mean and negative median returns at every
  entry time studied. Almeida/Freire/Hizmeri (CBOE 1-minute SPXW, 2012-2025): calls and puts
  both negative, worse out of the money, "similar across different times of the day".
  Dim/Eraker/Vilkov (SPXW 2012-2023): "all the strategies reap negative returns on average,
  with the distribution being extremely wide and right-skewed, especially later in the day."
  ATM straddle mean returns by entry: -0.7% at 10:00, -4.8% at 12:00, -21% at 14:30, -41% at 15:30.
- Costs dominate. Beckmeyer/Branger/Gayda (Cboe retail-flagged data): retail long positions lost
  53-61% of value; more than 60% of daily retail losses were transaction costs; effective retail
  spreads 6.1% on calls, 4.9% on puts. A 2025 order-flow paper puts the average 0DTE spread at
  about 9% of option price. Market makers, net short, are the documented winners.
- The variance risk premium exists but is tiny per day ("about 0.01%", Dim et al.), so neither
  side has a large structural edge after costs. Chong (HKUST): 0DTEs "are expensive but do not
  appear to be mispriced".
- Event days (FOMC, CPI) show large moves concentrated in one bar after the announcement, but no
  source shows positive after-cost expected returns for a naked long rule on those days.
- Spreads are partly avoidable: SEC DERA finds customer non-marketable limit orders sit at the
  best bid or offer more than half the time. This is the one documented lever on cost.

## 2. Practitioner rule candidates, ranked by evidence quality

| 1 | 5-minute opening range breakout (09:30-09:35), buy ATM call on break above / put on break below, one trade per day, +100% target, -50% stop, flat 15:30 | Backtest, 303 trades, Feb 2024-Mar 2026, 1-min option bars | None, mid fills | +59% on $25k, 41% win rate, 2:1 payoff | 105 parameter sets searched, Mon/Wed/Fri filter picked after the fact, bull sample |
| 2 | Long ATM 0DTE call, rules undisclosed | Backtest, SPX 2023-2026 | Mid plus 1 tick slippage, no commissions | 28% win rate, average -0.18R | Rules not replicable |
| 3 | End-of-day $1.00 call "lotto" held to expiry | Short sample, Oct 2023-Mar 2024 | None | ~15% win rate, cumulative P&L decayed | Author calls it preliminary |
| 4+ | VWAP momentum, trend continuation, SpotGamma regime plays, event straddles | Rules published, no data | None | Anecdotes only | Unverifiable |

Bottom line: no source that models bid/ask and fees shows positive expectancy for buying
single-leg 0DTE SPY/SPX options. The only positive result used mid fills, zero costs, and was
selected from many configurations.

## 3. Risk and execution practice

- Premium paid is the only hard cap on a long option. Common sizing guidance is 1-2% of account
  per trade; one contract for beginners (Saxo, practitioner guides).
- Final hour: gamma spikes, liquidity thins, option desks de-risk before the last 30 minutes,
  some clearing firms require 0DTE flat by 15:45 (Numerix). SPY options trade until 16:15 but
  settle physically; close long SPY 0DTE before 16:00.
- Daily loss limit: 0.5-3% of account or 2-3x per-trade risk; stop after about 3 consecutive losers.
- Execution: limit orders, marketable limit at the touch as the compromise. No source quantifies
  SPY 0DTE fills versus mid, so buy-at-ask, sell-at-bid stays the conservative assumption.
- Schwab fees: $0 base plus $0.65 per contract online. An undisclosed "Industry Fee" applies on
  sells (Section 31, FINRA TAF, ORF, OCC pass-through). Component rates sum to roughly $0.03-0.04
  per contract on a sell at these premiums; Schwab's actual charge is not published.

## 4. Tooling: what the open-source world does

- optopsy changed its default fill from mid to "spread" (buy at ask, sell at bid) in March 2026,
  calling mid "overly optimistic". Our default already matches.
- lambdaclass options_portfolio_backtester separates fill model from cost model and offers a
  size-aware fill. QuantConnect Lean fills at ask/bid with slippage added and models a per-order
  minimum fee.
- ROGUE-OPS, an open-source SPY 0DTE paper system with a kill switch and a daily-loss governor fed
  by realized fills, reports that every strategy its authors tested was negative.
- Worth borrowing: fill versus cost model split, explicit fill-assumption label on every result,
  "no bid/ask means no fill", a hashed journal line, an operator ARM step before a session, a data
  fidelity tag on every result, and recording raw Schwab level-one option fields for replay.

## 5. Recommendation

The honest expert answer: buying 0DTE options is a negative-expectancy activity after costs in
every dataset we could find, and a fixed 15% target does not change that arithmetic. The best
this project can do is test one hypothesis cleanly enough that it either survives honest fills
or is retired with evidence.

Hypothesis to test next (replaces Rule A once Schwab quotes flow):

- Entry: 5-minute opening range on SPY, 09:30-09:35 ET. First 1-minute close above the range
  high buys the nearest at-the-money call; first close below buys the nearest put. One entry per
  day. Entry window 09:36-11:00. Premium band and size gate from Rule A still apply.
- Exit: 15% net profit target on premium (the project hypothesis), 50% soft stop, hard exit 15:30.
- Shadow exit recorded offline from the same quote stream: +100% target, -50% stop, so the
  published rule's exit can be compared against ours without a second position.
- Costs: $0.65 per contract per side plus a $0.04 per contract regulatory placeholder on sells,
  ask-buy, bid-sell, one tick slippage. Fill assumption labeled on every result.
- Expectation going in: the published +59% was earned with mid fills and no costs. With a 41%
  win rate, honest fills on a $1.50 option cost roughly 5-8% of premium per round trip, which is
  most of the reported edge. We expect this to fail or be marginal. That is the point of testing.
- Stop condition: 40 forward paper sessions, or earlier if the daily loss governor trips 3 sessions
  in a row. Then decide to retire or refine, not re-search parameters.

Prerequisites, in order: Schwab quote adapter; record every quote to a daily file; SPY equity
quote polling from 09:30 to build the range; the ORB rule and tests; the regulatory fee on sells;
an ARM step and journal line hashes.
